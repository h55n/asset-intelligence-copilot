how it works

Ask. Get the answer and the evidence.

Ask in plain words

No dashboards to learn, no queries to write. "Which wells are down?" or "any late spill reports?" - the copilot works out which records to pull.

Rules, not guesses

The operating rules - producing thresholds, spill classes, filing deadlines - are read from the fleet manuals and applied by a deterministic engine. Numbers come from records, never from the model's memory.

It remembers corrections

Tell it "that reading was wrong, the real value is 41 °C" and it keeps the original, records who corrected it and why, and uses the correction from then on - at exactly the scope you meant.

what answers look like

The verdict first,
the proof attached.

Every answer names the records it used - inspection IDs, notes, manuals. Click any of them in the chat and the copilot pulls the full record. If two people tell it different things, it says so instead of quietly picking one.
